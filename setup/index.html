Where will it run?

The relay prepares the camera picture for your TV. This device needs to stay on while you want alerts.

Connect your LG

Open Developer Mode on the TV, turn on Key Server and enter the address shown there.

Check your television

Install the TV app

Use the same pairing to install Camera Overlay. Existing camera settings are kept.

Add a camera

Copy the RTSP or RTSPS address from your camera system. Use a medium-quality stream or substream to start.

Using UniFi Protect?

Open the camera’s settings in Protect and copy its supported RTSP/RTSPS stream address. A browser sharing link is different. Camera video does not itself provide person or doorbell alerts; those are selected in HA later.

A panel that fits your screen
